Contact Us

Get in Touch

Have a question about our services? Need help with your booking? We are here to assist you. Reach out and we will respond quickly.

How long does Aadhaar update take? ▼
After successful biometric verification at our center, Aadhaar updates are typically reflected within 30 to 90 days by UIDAI. We will guide you through the process and give you the acknowledgement slip.
What documents do I need to bring? ▼
Requirements vary by the type of update. For address update, bring proof of address (electricity bill, rent agreement, etc.). For name correction, bring a legal document with the correct name. Contact us for a complete list specific to your service.
Can I book for someone else? ▼
Yes. You can book a token for a family member. Just use their Aadhaar last 4 digits during booking. Please ensure the person physically attends the appointment as biometrics are required.
What is the service fee? ▼
Government-prescribed fees apply as set by UIDAI. Our service assistance charge is nominal. Please contact us directly for the current fee structure for your specific service type.
